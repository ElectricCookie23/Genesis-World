## Graphics overhaul

No external textures, models or images were added. Terrain detail, cloud noise, plant and creature models, imposters, skins and every material are generated by the code at load. Techniques follow published work: Tessendorf's FFT ocean, the pipe model for hydraulic erosion (Mei, Decaudin and Hu), Perlin-Worley cloud noise and Beer-powder lighting (Schneider), Cox and Munk sea-surface slope statistics, ground-truth ambient occlusion and temporal reprojection anti-aliasing as implemented in three.js (MIT licence).
